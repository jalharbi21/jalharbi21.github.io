Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

HOLLIS is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own Windows computer. This policy explains what data HOLLIS uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to the HOLLIS desktop app and to its optional connection to your Google account.

In short: HOLLIS has no server and no HOLLIS account. Your data stays on your PC, except for the pieces sent to the AI and voice services listed below so they can answer you. HOLLIS never sells your data, never uses it for advertising, and never sends an email or creates an event without your approval.

1. What HOLLIS runs on

HOLLIS runs entirely on your computer. The developer of HOLLIS does not operate a server that receives your conversations, emails, calendar or files, and does not have access to them.

2. Google user data

Connecting Google is optional. If you connect it, you sign in on Google's own page and choose to grant HOLLIS these permissions:

PermissionWhat HOLLIS does with it
gmail.readonlyReads, searches and summarizes your email when you ask (for example "any important emails?").
gmail.composeSaves reply drafts in your Gmail for you to review.
gmail.sendSends an email only after you approve it on an on-screen approval card.
calendar.eventsReads your upcoming events, and creates or changes an event only after you approve it.

HOLLIS uses Google user data only to provide the features you ask for. It does not read your mailbox in the background for any other purpose, and it does not delete email.

Limited Use

HOLLIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, HOLLIS:

  • uses Google user data only to provide and improve user-facing features you can see and use;
  • transfers it to others only as needed to provide those features (see section 4), for security, to comply with law, or with your consent;
  • does not use it for advertising, does not sell it, and does not let anyone read it except as described here;
  • does not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

3. Where your data is stored

  • Google sign-in token: saved only on your computer, in the HOLLIS folder (google/token.json). It is never uploaded by HOLLIS.
  • Email and calendar content: fetched when you ask and used to answer. Short summaries may be kept in HOLLIS's conversation history on your computer.
  • Conversations, notes and settings: stored on your computer.
  • Voice: wake-word detection runs on your computer. Speech is turned into text to answer you; HOLLIS does not keep a recording library.

4. Services HOLLIS sends data to

To answer you, HOLLIS sends the relevant text (your request and, when needed, the related email or calendar content) to the AI and voice services you have set up. Each processes it under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Anthropic (Claude): the main assistant that writes HOLLIS's answers.
  • Google (Gemini): a backup assistant used if Claude is unavailable.
  • OpenAI: real-time voice conversation, if enabled.
  • ElevenLabs: turns HOLLIS's answers into speech, so spoken answers (which may summarize your email) pass through it.
  • Web search services: receive search queries you ask for, not your email.

HOLLIS sends only what is needed for the request in front of it. No other parties receive your Google data.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google: remove HOLLIS at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete google/token.json from the HOLLIS folder.
  • Delete everything: delete the HOLLIS folder from your computer. Because data is stored locally, this removes it.
  • Approvals: you can decline any approval card; nothing is sent or created without your yes.

6. Security

Connections to Google and the services above use encrypted HTTPS. Keys and tokens stay on your computer. HOLLIS's own windows only load HOLLIS's own pages, and content from email or the web is treated as information, never as instructions to act.

7. Children

HOLLIS is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
