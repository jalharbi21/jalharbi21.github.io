A personal AI assistant that lives on your own PC.

HOLLIS is a voice assistant for Windows. You talk to it, it answers out loud, it shows ideas as 3D holograms, and it helps with email, calendar, research and planning. It runs on your computer, keeps your data there, and never sends an email or creates an event without your yes.

Voice

Talk to it

Say "Hey Hollis" and ask. It answers in a natural voice and remembers context within a conversation.

Holograms

See it

Engines, organs, buildings, data and business models appear as interactive 3D holograms on your desktop.

Knowledge

Expert library

Researched reference packs across science, engineering, medicine, law, business and every major industry.

Approval first
